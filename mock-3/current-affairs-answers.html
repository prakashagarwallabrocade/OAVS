Q.41
Alexander Zverev won his second Grand Slam title of 2026 at the US Open. Whom did he beat in the final?
  1. 1. Jannik Sinner
  2. 2. Carlos Alcaraz
  3. 3. Taylor Fritz
  4. 4. Ben Shelton
Answer: 4 — Zverev beat American Ben Shelton on 13 September 2026, after also winning the French Open that year.
Q.42
The FIDE Candidates Tournament 2026, won by Javokhir Sindarov to earn a match against D. Gukesh, was held in which country?
  1. 1. Cyprus
  2. 2. Canada
  3. 3. Spain
  4. 4. Uzbekistan
Answer: 1 — The 2026 Candidates was held in Cyprus. Sindarov won with a round to spare and without losing a game. Toronto (Canada) hosted the 2024 edition.
Q.43
Consider the following statements about the IPL 2026 final.
1. Royal Challengers Bengaluru beat Gujarat Titans to retain the title.
2. Virat Kohli was named Player of the Match.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
  1. 1. Only 1
  2. 2. Only 2
  3. 3. Both 1 and 2
  4. 4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: 3 — Both are correct. RCB won by 5 wickets at Ahmedabad on 31 May 2026, becoming only the third team to win back-to-back IPL titles.
Q.44
Who scored the only goal in the FIFA World Cup 2026 final between Spain and Argentina?
  1. 1. Ferran Torres
  2. 2. Lamine Yamal
  3. 3. Mikel Oyarzabal
  4. 4. Nico Williams
Answer: 1 — Substitute Ferran Torres scored in the 106th minute from Nico Williams's header. Spain won their second World Cup (after 2010).
Q.45
Consider the following statements about the ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026 final.
1. India's total of 255/5 was the highest ever in a T20 World Cup final.
2. Sanju Samson was named Player of the Match.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
  1. 1. Only 1
  2. 2. Only 2
  3. 3. Both 1 and 2
  4. 4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: 1 — Statement 1 is correct. Statement 2 is wrong: Samson top-scored with 89, but Jasprit Bumrah was Player of the Match.
Q.46
Who was the Player of the Match in the ICC Women's Cricket World Cup 2025 final, which India won?
  1. 1. Deepti Sharma
  2. 2. Shafali Verma
  3. 3. Smriti Mandhana
  4. 4. Jemimah Rodrigues
Answer: 2 — Shafali Verma scored 87 and took 2 wickets. Deepti Sharma was Player of the Tournament.
Q.47
At the BWF World Championships 2026 held in New Delhi, which country won its first-ever World Championship gold medals (men's singles and mixed doubles)?
  1. 1. Denmark
  2. 2. India
  3. 3. Thailand
  4. 4. France
Answer: 4 — Alex Lanier (men's singles) and Thom Gicquel/Delphine Delrue (mixed doubles) won France's first world titles. An Se Young of Korea won the women's singles.
Q.48
The FIH Hockey World Cup 2026 (August 2026) was co-hosted by which two countries?
  1. 1. India and Malaysia
  2. 2. Germany and Spain
  3. 3. Australia and New Zealand
  4. 4. Belgium and the Netherlands
Answer: 4 — Belgium (Wavre) and the Netherlands (Amstelveen) hosted it. Bhubaneswar and Rourkela hosted the 2023 men's edition.
Q.49
India won 85 medals at the Asian Games 2026. How many of them were gold?
  1. 1. 28
  2. 2. 21
  3. 3. 16
  4. 4. 25
Answer: 2 — India won 21 gold, 27 silver and 37 bronze and finished fourth. It had won 28 golds at Hangzhou 2023.
Q.50
Consider the following statements about the National Sports Awards 2025, announced in August 2026.
1. No sportsperson was named for the Major Dhyan Chand Khel Ratna Award.
2. Chess player Divya Deshmukh was among the Arjuna Award recipients.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
  1. 1. Only 1
  2. 2. Only 2
  3. 3. Both 1 and 2
  4. 4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: 3 — Both are correct. The selection committee was chaired by Justice (Retd.) Arun Kumar Mishra, and 17 Arjuna Awards were announced.
Q.51
How much public capital expenditure was proposed in the Union Budget 2026-27?
  1. 1. ₹11.21 lakh crore
  2. 2. ₹10 lakh crore
  3. 3. ₹12.2 lakh crore
  4. 4. ₹15 lakh crore
Answer: 3 — Capex of ₹12.2 lakh crore was proposed, up from the 2025-26 budget estimate of ₹11.21 lakh crore.
Q.52
Under the VB-G RAM G Act (which replaced MGNREGA), how is funding shared between the Centre and the North-Eastern and Himalayan States?
  1. 1. 60:40
  2. 2. 90:10
  3. 3. 75:25
  4. 4. 50:50
Answer: 2 — The general ratio is 60:40, with 90:10 for the North-Eastern and Himalayan States. The Act guarantees 125 days of work.
Q.53
In March 2026, the National Dental Commission was constituted, replacing which body?
  1. 1. Medical Council of India
  2. 2. Dental Council of India
  3. 3. Indian Dental Association
  4. 4. National Board of Examinations
Answer: 2 — It follows the model of the National Medical Commission, which replaced the MCI in 2020.
Q.54
How were the 131 Padma awards announced in January 2026 divided?
  1. 1. 7 Padma Vibhushan, 19 Padma Bhushan, 105 Padma Shri
  2. 2. 4 Padma Vibhushan, 10 Padma Bhushan, 117 Padma Shri
  3. 3. 5 Padma Vibhushan, 13 Padma Bhushan, 113 Padma Shri
  4. 4. 6 Padma Vibhushan, 15 Padma Bhushan, 110 Padma Shri
Answer: 3 — There were 5 + 13 + 113 = 131 awards, including 19 women and 16 posthumous awardees.
Q.55
'Taiwan Travelogue' by Yáng Shuāng-zǐ, translated by Lin King, won the International Booker Prize 2026. What made this win historic?
  1. 1. It was the first book by a Taiwanese man to win
  2. 2. It was the first book originally written in Mandarin Chinese to win
  3. 3. It was the first non-fiction book to win
  4. 4. It was the first book translated from Japanese to win
Answer: 2 — It is the first Mandarin-language winner of the International Booker. The novel is set in Japanese-ruled Taiwan in the 1930s.
Q.56
For which discovery did Mary Brunkow, Fred Ramsdell and Shimon Sakaguchi receive the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine 2025?
  1. 1. Peripheral immune tolerance (regulatory T cells)
  2. 2. microRNA and gene regulation
  3. 3. mRNA vaccine technology
  4. 4. Temperature and touch receptors
Answer: 1 — They explained how regulatory T cells stop the immune system from attacking the body. microRNA won in 2024, mRNA in 2023, and temperature and touch receptors in 2021.
Q.57
László Krasznahorkai, winner of the Nobel Prize in Literature 2025, is a novelist from which country?
  1. 1. Hungary
  2. 2. Poland
  3. 3. Romania
  4. 4. Austria
Answer: 1 — Krasznahorkai is Hungarian, known for 'Sátántangó' and 'The Melancholy of Resistance'.
Q.58
Khaled El-Enany, who took office as Director-General of UNESCO in November 2025, is from which country?
  1. 1. Morocco
  2. 2. Jordan
  3. 3. Saudi Arabia
  4. 4. Egypt
Answer: 4 — The Egyptologist and former antiquities minister is the first UNESCO chief from the Arab world. He was elected at the General Conference in Samarkand.
Q.59
Who was elected the 10th President of Ireland in October 2025?
  1. 1. Mary McAleese
  2. 2. Heather Humphreys
  3. 3. Mary Lou McDonald
  4. 4. Catherine Connolly
Answer: 4 — Independent left-wing candidate Catherine Connolly won about 63% of the vote, succeeding Michael D. Higgins.
Q.60
José Antonio Kast won the presidential run-off election in December 2025 in which country?
  1. 1. Argentina
  2. 2. Peru
  3. 3. Chile
  4. 4. Colombia
Answer: 3 — The conservative Kast defeated Jeannette Jara with about 58% of the vote.
Q.61
Which country holds the presidency of the G20 in 2026?
  1. 1. South Africa
  2. 2. Brazil
  3. 3. India
  4. 4. United States
Answer: 4 — The USA took over from South Africa, which hosted the first G20 summit in Africa (Johannesburg, November 2025).
Q.62
Who became Nepal's first woman Prime Minister, leading an interim government from September 2025?
  1. 1. Bidya Devi Bhandari
  2. 2. Sahana Pradhan
  3. 3. Arzu Rana Deuba
  4. 4. Sushila Karki
Answer: 4 — Former Chief Justice Sushila Karki led the interim government until the March 2026 election, after which Balendra Shah became PM.
Q.63
INS Mahendragiri, commissioned at Visakhapatnam in July 2026, belongs to which class of warship?
  1. 1. Project 15B destroyer
  2. 2. Scorpene-class submarine
  3. 3. Project 17A stealth frigate
  4. 4. Aircraft carrier
Answer: 3 — Mahendragiri (named after the Odisha peak) is a Project 17A (Nilgiri-class) stealth frigate built by Mazagon Dock.
Q.64
'Veer Guardian 2026', held at Air Force Station Jodhpur in September 2026, was the first bilateral air exercise between India and which country?
  1. 1. France
  2. 2. Australia
  3. 3. United States
  4. 4. Japan
Answer: 4 — The Indian Air Force and the Japan Air Self-Defense Force held their first bilateral air exercise, Veer Guardian, in 2026.
Q.65
In the World Economic Forum's Global Gender Gap Index 2026, what was India's rank?
  1. 1. 127
  2. 2. 142
  3. 3. 118
  4. 4. 131
Answer: 4 — India stayed at 131st, with 64.5% parity. Iceland topped the index again.
Q.66
In the RSF World Press Freedom Index 2026, what was India's rank out of 180 countries?
  1. 1. 157
  2. 2. 151
  3. 3. 161
  4. 4. 142
Answer: 1 — India slipped from 151st (2025) to 157th and is in the 'very serious' category.
Q.67
Who is the Chief Election Commissioner of India (as of October 2026)?
  1. 1. Rajiv Kumar
  2. 2. Sushil Chandra
  3. 3. Gyanesh Kumar
  4. 4. Sukhbir Singh Sandhu
Answer: 3 — Gyanesh Kumar became CEC in February 2025, the first appointed under the 2023 Act on the appointment of the CEC and other ECs.
Q.68
Odisha Chief Minister Mohan Charan Majhi represents which Assembly constituency?
  1. 1. Hinjili
  2. 2. Champua
  3. 3. Patna (Keonjhar)
  4. 4. Keonjhar
Answer: 4 — Mohan Charan Majhi is the MLA from Keonjhar. Hinjili was former CM Naveen Patnaik's seat.
Q.69
What financial support does the 'Mukhyamantri Kanya Sumangal Yojana', announced in the Odisha Budget 2026-27, provide?
  1. 1. A ₹20,000 savings instrument at a girl's birth and up to ₹1,01,001 on graduation
  2. 2. ₹10,000 a year to all women aged 21–60
  3. 3. Free bicycles to all Class IX girls
  4. 4. ₹50,000 marriage assistance
Answer: 1 — The scheme promotes girls' education from birth to graduation. ₹10,000 a year to women aged 21–60 is the Subhadra Yojana.
Q.70
The Odisha Cabinet approved the 'Odisha Jan Vishwas Bill, 2026'. What is the main purpose of such a law?
  1. 1. Decriminalising minor offences in state laws to improve ease of living and doing business
  2. 2. Giving land rights to forest dwellers
  3. 3. Making voting compulsory in local elections
  4. 4. Creating a state-level Lokayukta
Answer: 1 — Like the central Jan Vishwas Act (2023), it replaces jail terms for minor, technical violations with fines. It is the second phase of Odisha's regulatory reforms.
