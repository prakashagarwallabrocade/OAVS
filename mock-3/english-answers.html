Q.1
The following sentence has been split into four segments. Identify the segment that contains a grammatical error.
The committee / have submitted / its report / to the principal.
  1. 1. The committee
  2. 2. have submitted
  3. 3. its report
  4. 4. to the principal.
Answer: 2 — The committee acts as one body (shown by 'its'), so the verb must be singular: 'has submitted'.
Q.2
The following sentence has been split into four segments. Identify the segment that contains a grammatical error.
She is / more cleverer / than her / elder sister.
  1. 1. She is
  2. 2. than her
  3. 3. elder sister.
  4. 4. more cleverer
Answer: 4 — A double comparative is wrong: it should be 'cleverer' or 'more clever', not 'more cleverer'.
Q.3
The following sentence has been split into four segments. Identify the segment that contains a grammatical error.
Despite of / the heavy rain, / the match / continued.
  1. 1. the heavy rain,
  2. 2. Despite of
  3. 3. the match
  4. 4. continued.
Answer: 2 — 'Despite' is not followed by 'of'. Use 'Despite the heavy rain' or 'In spite of the heavy rain'.
Q.4
Select the most appropriate option to fill in the blank.
He was accused ______ theft by his employer.
  1. 1. for
  2. 2. with
  3. 3. of
  4. 4. about
Answer: 3 — The correct pattern is 'accuse someone of something'. ('Charged with' takes 'with'.)
Q.5
Select the most appropriate option to fill in the blank.
By the time we reached the station, the train ______.
  1. 1. left
  2. 2. has left
  3. 3. was leaving
  4. 4. had left
Answer: 4 — The earlier of two past actions takes the past perfect: the train had left before we reached.
Q.6
Select the most appropriate option to fill in the blank.
Not only the students but also the teacher ______ present at the ceremony.
  1. 1. were
  2. 2. are
  3. 3. have been
  4. 4. was
Answer: 4 — With 'not only … but also', the verb agrees with the nearer subject, 'the teacher', which is singular.
Q.7
Select the most appropriate synonym of the given word.
Pragmatic
  1. 1. Idealistic
  2. 2. Dogmatic
  3. 3. Practical
  4. 4. Romantic
Answer: 3 — Pragmatic means dealing with things sensibly and realistically, i.e. practical.
Q.8
Select the most appropriate synonym of the given word.
Vindicate
  1. 1. Exonerate
  2. 2. Accuse
  3. 3. Condemn
  4. 4. Punish
Answer: 1 — Vindicate means to clear of blame, i.e. exonerate.
Q.9
Select the most appropriate antonym of the given word.
Benign
  1. 1. Harmless
  2. 2. Kind
  3. 3. Malignant
  4. 4. Gentle
Answer: 3 — Benign means gentle or harmless. Malignant (harmful, evil) is the opposite.
Q.10
Select the most appropriate antonym of the given word.
Ostentatious
  1. 1. Showy
  2. 2. Flamboyant
  3. 3. Pretentious
  4. 4. Modest
Answer: 4 — Ostentatious means showy and designed to impress. Modest is the opposite.
Q.11
Select the correctly spelt word.
  1. 1. Maintainance
  2. 2. Maintenence
  3. 3. Maintenance
  4. 4. Mantainance
Answer: 3 — 'Maintenance' drops the 'ai' of 'maintain'.
Q.12
Select the INCORRECTLY spelt word.
  1. 1. Privilege
  2. 2. Rhythm
  3. 3. Necessary
  4. 4. Harrass
Answer: 4 — The correct spelling is 'harass' (one 'r', double 's').
Q.13
Select the most appropriate meaning of the given idiom.
To burn one's boats
  1. 1. To waste one's money
  2. 2. To destroy enemy property
  3. 3. To do something that makes it impossible to go back
  4. 4. To work late into the night
Answer: 3 — It means to cut off every means of retreat and commit oneself fully.
Q.14
Select the most appropriate meaning of the given idiom.
To take something with a pinch of salt
  1. 1. To make it more interesting
  2. 2. To accept it with some doubt
  3. 3. To accept it completely
  4. 4. To reject it angrily
Answer: 2 — Taking something with a pinch of salt means not believing it completely.
Q.15
Select the option that can be used as a one-word substitute for the given group of words.
The killing of one's own mother
  1. 1. Patricide
  2. 2. Fratricide
  3. 3. Regicide
  4. 4. Matricide
Answer: 4 — Matricide = killing one's mother. Patricide = father, fratricide = brother, regicide = king.
Q.16
Select the option that can be used as a one-word substitute for the given group of words.
A list of items to be discussed at a meeting
  1. 1. Agenda
  2. 2. Minutes
  3. 3. Memorandum
  4. 4. Itinerary
Answer: 1 — The agenda lists the business of a meeting. The minutes are its record, and an itinerary is a travel plan.
Q.17
Select the most appropriate option to improve the underlined part of the sentence. If no improvement is needed, select 'No improvement'.
He has returned back from Delhi.
  1. 1. come back again
  2. 2. returned
  3. 3. returned again
  4. 4. No improvement
Answer: 2 — 'Return' already means 'come back', so 'returned back' is redundant.
Q.18
Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice.
Somebody has stolen my pen.
  1. 1. My pen was stolen by somebody.
  2. 2. My pen had been stolen.
  3. 3. My pen has been stolen.
  4. 4. My pen is stolen by somebody.
Answer: 3 — Present perfect active becomes 'has/have been + past participle'. A vague agent like 'somebody' is usually dropped.
Q.19
Select the option that expresses the given sentence in indirect speech.
She said to me, "Do you know the way to the station?"
  1. 1. She asked me if I knew the way to the station.
  2. 2. She asked me do I know the way to the station.
  3. 3. She said to me that did I know the way to the station.
  4. 4. She asked me whether did I know the way to the station.
Answer: 1 — Yes/no questions are reported with 'if/whether', statement word order and a back-shifted tense: 'if I knew'.
Q.20
The given sentence is divided into parts. The first part is fixed. Arrange the remaining parts in the correct order.
Utkal Divas
P: to mark the formation
Q: is celebrated
R: of Odisha as a separate province.
S: on 1 April
  1. 1. QPRS
  2. 2. QSPR
  3. 3. SQPR
  4. 4. PRQS
Answer: 2 — Utkal Divas is celebrated (Q) on 1 April (S) to mark the formation (P) of Odisha as a separate province (R).
