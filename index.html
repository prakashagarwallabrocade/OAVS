Marking scheme

  • Every question has four options and carries 1 mark.
  • 0.25 mark is deducted for every wrong answer. Unattempted questions score zero.
  • Score = Correct − 0.25 × Wrong.

How to use these mocks

  1. Take the online test from a mock page: a countdown of 3 hours, automatic scoring with negative marking, and a full answer review.
  2. Or download the question paper PDF and attempt it on paper in one sitting of 3 hours.
  3. For the paper version, use the one-page answer grid in the answer sheet PDF to score yourself.
  4. Read the explanations for every question you got wrong or guessed.
