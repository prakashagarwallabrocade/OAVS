Q.41
In November 2025, India won its first-ever ICC Women's Cricket World Cup by defeating which team in the final at Navi Mumbai?
  1. 1. Australia
  2. 2. South Africa
  3. 3. England
  4. 4. New Zealand
Answer: 2 — India beat South Africa by 52 runs in the final at the D.Y. Patil Stadium, Navi Mumbai, on 2 November 2025, winning their maiden Women's ODI World Cup.
Q.42
Who was named Player of the Tournament at the ICC Women's Cricket World Cup 2025?
  1. 1. Shafali Verma
  2. 2. Deepti Sharma
  3. 3. Smriti Mandhana
  4. 4. Harmanpreet Kaur
Answer: 2 — Deepti Sharma (215 runs and 22 wickets) was Player of the Tournament. Shafali Verma was Player of the Match in the final.
Q.43
India won the ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026 by defeating which team in the final at Ahmedabad on 8 March 2026?
  1. 1. New Zealand
  2. 2. South Africa
  3. 3. England
  4. 4. Australia
Answer: 1 — India beat New Zealand by 96 runs at the Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad. India became the first team to win consecutive T20 World Cups and the first to win one at home.
Q.44
Which franchise won the IPL 2026 title, defending the title it had won in 2025?
  1. 1. Gujarat Titans
  2. 2. Royal Challengers Bengaluru
  3. 3. Mumbai Indians
  4. 4. Punjab Kings
Answer: 2 — Royal Challengers Bengaluru beat Gujarat Titans by 5 wickets in the final at Ahmedabad on 31 May 2026, winning back-to-back titles.
Q.45
Which country won the FIFA World Cup 2026, held in the USA, Canada and Mexico?
  1. 1. Argentina
  2. 2. France
  3. 3. Spain
  4. 4. Brazil
Answer: 3 — Spain beat defending champions Argentina 1–0 after extra time in the final at East Rutherford, New Jersey, on 19 July 2026. It was Spain's second title (after 2010).
Q.46
Who won the women's singles title at Wimbledon 2026?
  1. 1. Karolína Muchová
  2. 2. Aryna Sabalenka
  3. 3. Linda Nosková
  4. 4. Iga Świątek
Answer: 3 — Czech player Linda Nosková beat fellow Czech Karolína Muchová in the final. Jannik Sinner won the men's singles.
Q.47
The 20th Asian Games (2026) were hosted by which country?
  1. 1. Japan
  2. 2. China
  3. 3. South Korea
  4. 4. Saudi Arabia
Answer: 1 — The 2026 Asian Games were held in Aichi–Nagoya, Japan. The previous edition (2023) was held in Hangzhou, China.
Q.48
How many medals did India win at the Asian Games 2026 in Aichi–Nagoya?
  1. 1. 106
  2. 2. 70
  3. 3. 85
  4. 4. 107
Answer: 3 — India won 85 medals (21 gold, 27 silver, 37 bronze) and finished fourth. This is India's second-best haul after the record 106 at Hangzhou 2023.
Q.49
The 2026 Commonwealth Games were held in which city?
  1. 1. Glasgow
  2. 2. Birmingham
  3. 3. Victoria
  4. 4. Gold Coast
Answer: 1 — Glasgow (Scotland) hosted the 2026 Commonwealth Games. Birmingham hosted in 2022 and Gold Coast in 2018.
Q.50
Which Indian city was chosen in November 2025 to host the centenary edition of the Commonwealth Games in 2030?
  1. 1. New Delhi
  2. 2. Bhubaneswar
  3. 3. Ahmedabad
  4. 4. Hyderabad
Answer: 3 — The Commonwealth Sport General Assembly awarded the 2030 Games to Ahmedabad. India last hosted the Games in Delhi in 2010.
Q.51
The Nobel Prize in Literature 2026 was awarded to which writer?
  1. 1. László Krasznahorkai
  2. 2. Anne Carson
  3. 3. Han Kang
  4. 4. Margaret Atwood
Answer: 2 — Canadian poet and classicist Anne Carson won the 2026 prize. László Krasznahorkai won in 2025 and Han Kang in 2024.
Q.52
The Nobel Peace Prize 2025 was awarded to María Corina Machado for her struggle for democracy in which country?
  1. 1. Colombia
  2. 2. Cuba
  3. 3. Venezuela
  4. 4. Nicaragua
Answer: 3 — Venezuelan opposition leader María Corina Machado received the 2025 Nobel Peace Prize.
Q.53
Joel Mokyr, Philippe Aghion and Peter Howitt received the 2025 Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences for their work on which subject?
  1. 1. Innovation-driven economic growth
  2. 2. Poverty alleviation experiments
  3. 3. Behavioural economics
  4. 4. Auction theory
Answer: 1 — They were honoured for explaining innovation-driven economic growth, including Aghion and Howitt's theory of growth through 'creative destruction'.
Q.54
Which novel won the Booker Prize 2025?
  1. 1. Orbital — Samantha Harvey
  2. 2. Prophet Song — Paul Lynch
  3. 3. Flesh — David Szalay
  4. 4. Heart Lamp — Banu Mushtaq
Answer: 3 — David Szalay won the 2025 Booker Prize for 'Flesh'. 'Heart Lamp' by Banu Mushtaq won the 2025 International Booker Prize.
Q.55
The 18th BRICS Summit was held in September 2026 in which city?
  1. 1. Rio de Janeiro
  2. 2. New Delhi
  3. 3. Kazan
  4. 4. Johannesburg
Answer: 2 — India hosted the 18th BRICS Summit at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, on 12–13 September 2026. This was India's fourth BRICS chairship. Rio de Janeiro hosted in 2025 and Kazan in 2024.
Q.56
In its policy review of 7 October 2026, the RBI's Monetary Policy Committee raised the repo rate to what level?
  1. 1. 5.50%
  2. 2. 5.25%
  3. 3. 6.00%
  4. 4. 6.50%
Answer: 1 — The MPC raised the repo rate by 25 basis points from 5.25% to 5.50%, its first hike since February 2023.
Q.57
The VB-G RAM G Act, which replaced MGNREGA from 1 July 2026, guarantees how many days of wage employment per rural household in a financial year?
  1. 1. 100 days
  2. 2. 125 days
  3. 3. 150 days
  4. 4. 120 days
Answer: 2 — The Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act guarantees 125 days of work, up from MGNREGA's 100 days.
Q.58
Which site was inscribed in July 2026 as India's 45th UNESCO World Heritage Site?
  1. 1. Majuli River Island
  2. 2. Ancient Buddhist Site of Sarnath
  3. 3. Lingaraj Temple, Bhubaneswar
  4. 4. Kamakhya Temple
Answer: 2 — Sarnath (Uttar Pradesh), where the Buddha gave his first sermon, was inscribed at the 48th World Heritage Committee session in Busan, South Korea, on 25 July 2026.
Q.59
Who took oath as the 53rd Chief Justice of India on 24 November 2025?
  1. 1. Justice Surya Kant
  2. 2. Justice B.R. Gavai
  3. 3. Justice Sanjiv Khanna
  4. 4. Justice Vikram Nath
Answer: 1 — Justice Surya Kant succeeded Justice B.R. Gavai as the 53rd CJI.
Q.60
Who is the 15th Vice-President of India?
  1. 1. Jagdeep Dhankhar
  2. 2. C.P. Radhakrishnan
  3. 3. M. Venkaiah Naidu
  4. 4. Om Birla
Answer: 2 — C.P. Radhakrishnan took office as the 15th Vice-President on 12 September 2025, succeeding Jagdeep Dhankhar.
Q.61
Who became the first BJP Chief Minister of West Bengal after the 2026 Assembly elections?
  1. 1. Sukanta Majumdar
  2. 2. Dilip Ghosh
  3. 3. Suvendu Adhikari
  4. 4. Samik Bhattacharya
Answer: 3 — The BJP won a large majority in West Bengal in 2026, and Suvendu Adhikari was sworn in as Chief Minister in May 2026. He defeated Mamata Banerjee in Bhabanipur.
Q.62
Who became the first BJP leader to be Chief Minister of Bihar in 2026, after Nitish Kumar stepped down to move to the Rajya Sabha?
  1. 1. Vijay Kumar Sinha
  2. 2. Nityanand Rai
  3. 3. Giriraj Singh
  4. 4. Samrat Choudhary
Answer: 4 — Samrat Choudhary was sworn in as Bihar's Chief Minister in April 2026, the first from the BJP.
Q.63
The chief guests at India's Republic Day 2026 celebrations were the top leaders of which organisation?
  1. 1. European Union
  2. 2. ASEAN
  3. 3. African Union
  4. 4. BIMSTEC
Answer: 1 — European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen and European Council President António Costa were joint chief guests. The India–EU Free Trade Agreement was concluded on 27 January 2026 during their visit.
Q.64
Which Indian astronaut, the first Indian to visit the International Space Station, received the Ashoka Chakra on Republic Day 2026?
  1. 1. Rakesh Sharma
  2. 2. Shubhanshu Shukla
  3. 3. Prasanth Nair
  4. 4. Ajit Krishnan
Answer: 2 — Group Captain Shubhanshu Shukla flew to the ISS on the Axiom-4 mission in 2025 and was awarded the Ashoka Chakra, India's highest peacetime gallantry award.
Q.65
The G20 Leaders' Summit of November 2025, the first held on the African continent, took place in which city?
  1. 1. Johannesburg
  2. 2. Cairo
  3. 3. Nairobi
  4. 4. Addis Ababa
Answer: 1 — South Africa hosted the G20 summit in Johannesburg on 22–23 November 2025.
Q.66
COP30, the UN Climate Change Conference held in November 2025, took place in which city?
  1. 1. Baku, Azerbaijan
  2. 2. Belém, Brazil
  3. 3. Dubai, UAE
  4. 4. Bonn, Germany
Answer: 2 — COP30 was held in Belém in the Brazilian Amazon. COP29 was in Baku (2024) and COP28 in Dubai (2023).
Q.67
Who became Japan's first woman Prime Minister in October 2025?
  1. 1. Sanae Takaichi
  2. 2. Yuriko Koike
  3. 3. Seiko Noda
  4. 4. Yoko Kamikawa
Answer: 1 — Sanae Takaichi, leader of the Liberal Democratic Party, became Japan's first female Prime Minister on 21 October 2025.
Q.68
From 21 November 2025, the Government of India brought into force how many Labour Codes, replacing 29 central labour laws?
  1. 1. Three
  2. 2. Four
  3. 3. Five
  4. 4. Six
Answer: 2 — Four codes came into force: Wages, Industrial Relations, Social Security, and Occupational Safety, Health & Working Conditions.
Q.69
What was the size of the Odisha Budget 2026-27 presented by Chief Minister Mohan Charan Majhi in February 2026?
  1. 1. ₹3.10 lakh crore
  2. 2. ₹2.90 lakh crore
  3. 3. ₹2.65 lakh crore
  4. 4. ₹3.50 lakh crore
Answer: 1 — The 2026-27 budget was ₹3.10 lakh crore, up from ₹2.90 lakh crore in 2025-26, with a 12% nominal GSDP growth target.

  1. 1. Hari Babu Kambhampati
  2. 2. Raghubar Das
  3. 3. Ganeshi Lal
  4. 4. Biswabhusan Harichandan
