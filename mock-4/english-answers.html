Q.1
The following sentence has been split into four segments. Identify the segment that contains a grammatical error.
Scarcely had / he entered the hall / than the audience / rose to greet him.
  1. 1. Scarcely had
  2. 2. he entered the hall
  3. 3. than the audience
  4. 4. rose to greet him.
Answer: 3 — 'Scarcely' and 'hardly' are followed by 'when' or 'before', not 'than': 'Scarcely had he entered the hall when the audience rose …'.
Q.2
The following sentence has been split into four segments. Identify the segment that contains a grammatical error.
The poor / is / often exploited / by moneylenders.
  1. 1. is
  2. 2. The poor
  3. 3. often exploited
  4. 4. by moneylenders.
Answer: 1 — 'The + adjective' (the poor, the rich) refers to a class of people and is plural: 'The poor are often exploited'.
Q.3
The following sentence has been split into four segments. Identify the segment that contains a grammatical error.
The headmaster, as well as / the teachers, / are / responsible for discipline.
  1. 1. are
  2. 2. The headmaster, as well as
  3. 3. the teachers,
  4. 4. responsible for discipline.
Answer: 1 — With 'as well as', the verb agrees with the first subject, 'the headmaster', which is singular: 'is responsible'.
Q.4
Select the most appropriate option to fill in the blank.
The cattle ______ grazing peacefully in the meadow.
  1. 1. is
  2. 2. was
  3. 3. are
  4. 4. has been
Answer: 3 — 'Cattle' is always plural and takes a plural verb.
Q.5
Select the most appropriate option to fill in the blank.
Ten kilometres ______ too long a distance to walk in this heat.
  1. 1. are
  2. 2. were
  3. 3. have been
  4. 4. is
Answer: 4 — A plural amount of distance, money or time taken as a single quantity takes a singular verb.
Q.6
Select the most appropriate option to fill in the blank.
Many people in the village died ______ cholera during the epidemic.
  1. 1. from
  2. 2. of
  3. 3. by
  4. 4. with
Answer: 2 — We say 'die of' a disease and 'die from' a wound or injury.
Q.7
Select the most appropriate synonym of the given word.
Equanimity
  1. 1. Equality
  2. 2. Composure
  3. 3. Anxiety
  4. 4. Hostility
Answer: 2 — Equanimity means calmness and composure, especially in difficult situations. It is not related to 'equality'.
Q.8
Select the most appropriate synonym of the given word.
Perfidious
  1. 1. Perfect
  2. 2. Loyal
  3. 3. Treacherous
  4. 4. Generous
Answer: 3 — Perfidious means deceitful and untrustworthy, i.e. treacherous.
Q.9
Select the most appropriate antonym of the given word.
Loquacious
  1. 1. Talkative
  2. 2. Garrulous
  3. 3. Taciturn
  4. 4. Voluble
Answer: 3 — Loquacious means very talkative. Taciturn (reserved in speech) is the opposite. The other three are synonyms.
Q.10
Select the most appropriate antonym of the given word.
Parsimonious
  1. 1. Lavish
  2. 2. Stingy
  3. 3. Frugal
  4. 4. Miserly
Answer: 1 — Parsimonious means extremely unwilling to spend. Lavish is the opposite.
Q.11
Select the correctly spelt word.
  1. 1. Conoisseur
  2. 2. Connoiseur
  3. 3. Connoisseur
  4. 4. Connoissuer
Answer: 3 — The correct spelling is con-nois-seur (double n, double s).
Q.12
Select the INCORRECTLY spelt word.
  1. 1. Pronunciation
  2. 2. Mischievious
  3. 3. Accommodate
  4. 4. Liaison
Answer: 2 — The correct form is 'mischievous'. There is no 'i' before '-ous'.
Q.13
Select the most appropriate meaning of the given idiom.
To cry wolf
  1. 1. To raise a false alarm
  2. 2. To weep loudly
  3. 3. To attack suddenly
  4. 4. To complain about food
Answer: 1 — From Aesop's fable: repeatedly raising false alarms means people stop believing you.
Q.14
Select the most appropriate meaning of the given idiom.
Achilles' heel
  1. 1. A brave warrior
  2. 2. A painful injury
  3. 3. A strong foundation
  4. 4. A weak or vulnerable point
Answer: 4 — In Greek legend, Achilles' only vulnerable spot was his heel.
Q.15
Select the option that can be used as a one-word substitute for the given group of words.
A person who can speak many languages
  1. 1. Bilingual
  2. 2. Interpreter
  3. 3. Lexicographer
  4. 4. Polyglot
Answer: 4 — A polyglot knows many languages. Bilingual means two languages, and a lexicographer compiles dictionaries.
Q.16
Select the option that can be used as a one-word substitute for the given group of words.
An abnormal fear of heights
  1. 1. Claustrophobia
  2. 2. Hydrophobia
  3. 3. Acrophobia
  4. 4. Agoraphobia
Answer: 3 — Acrophobia = fear of heights. Claustrophobia = closed spaces, hydrophobia = water, agoraphobia = open or crowded places.
Q.17
Select the most appropriate option to improve the underlined part of the sentence. If no improvement is needed, select 'No improvement'.
I prefer to walk than to ride in this weather.
  1. 1. than ride
  2. 2. rather than ride
  3. 3. to riding
  4. 4. No improvement
Answer: 2 — 'Prefer to + verb' is followed by 'rather than + bare infinitive': 'I prefer to walk rather than ride'.
Q.18
Select the option that expresses the given sentence in passive voice.
Who taught you French?
  1. 1. By whom was you taught French?
  2. 2. Who was taught French by you?
  3. 3. By whom were you taught French?
  4. 4. By whom French was taught to you?
Answer: 3 — 'Who' becomes 'By whom'. The question order and the subject 'you' take 'were': 'By whom were you taught French?'.
Q.19
Select the option that expresses the given sentence in indirect speech.
The old man said, "Alas! I am ruined."
  1. 1. The old man said alas that he is ruined.
  2. 2. The old man exclaimed that alas he was ruined.
  3. 3. The old man said sadly that I was ruined.
  4. 4. The old man exclaimed sadly that he was ruined.
Answer: 4 — Exclamations are reported with 'exclaimed' plus an adverb (sadly, with sorrow). The interjection is dropped and the tense shifts back.
Q.20
The given sentence is divided into parts. The first part is fixed. Arrange the remaining parts in the correct order.
Chandrayaan-3
P: near the lunar south pole
Q: made India
R: the first country to land
S: in August 2023.
  1. 1. RQPS
  2. 2. QPRS
  3. 3. QRPS
  4. 4. SQRP
Answer: 3 — Chandrayaan-3 made India (Q) the first country to land (R) near the lunar south pole (P) in August 2023 (S).
