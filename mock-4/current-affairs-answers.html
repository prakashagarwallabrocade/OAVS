Q.41
Elena Rybakina won the women's singles title at the Australian Open 2026. Whom did she defeat in the final, and which country does she represent?
  1. 1. Iga Świątek; Kazakhstan
  2. 2. Aryna Sabalenka; Russia
  3. 3. Coco Gauff; Kazakhstan
  4. 4. Aryna Sabalenka; Kazakhstan
Answer: 4 — Rybakina, who plays for Kazakhstan, beat Aryna Sabalenka in the final.
Q.42
China retained the Thomas Cup in 2026, winning it for the 12th time. Which country did it beat in the final?
  1. 1. France
  2. 2. Indonesia
  3. 3. Denmark
  4. 4. India
Answer: 1 — China beat France 3–1 at Horsens, Denmark (April–May 2026). India had won the Thomas Cup in 2022.
Q.43
Which country won the Open section gold at the 46th Chess Olympiad (2026) in Samarkand, its second Olympiad title?
  1. 1. India
  2. 2. Ukraine
  3. 3. Uzbekistan
  4. 4. United States
Answer: 3 — Uzbekistan won at home after its first title at Chennai 2022. India took silver.
Q.44
Which Indian athlete was named the women's flag-bearer for the closing ceremony of the Asian Games 2026, after winning two gold medals and a silver?
  1. 1. Neeru Dhanda (trap shooting)
  2. 2. Manu Bhaker (pistol shooting)
  3. 3. Jyothi Yarraji (hurdles)
  4. 4. Lovlina Borgohain (boxing)
Answer: 1 — Trap shooter Neeru Dhanda was chosen for the closing ceremony. Manu Bhaker carried the flag at the opening ceremony.
Q.45

  1. 1. Pakistan
  2. 2. South Korea
  3. 3. Malaysia
  4. 4. Japan
Answer: 3 — The 5–1 win over Malaysia lifted India above Uzbekistan in the medal table, as India had more silvers.
Q.46
The BWF World Badminton Championships 2026 were held in which Indian city, returning to India after 17 years?
  1. 1. Hyderabad
  2. 2. New Delhi
  3. 3. Lucknow
  4. 4. Bhubaneswar
Answer: 2 — The championships were held at the Indira Gandhi Indoor Stadium, New Delhi (17–23 August 2026). Hyderabad hosted in 2009.
Q.47
Tommy Koh, a 2026 Ramon Magsaysay Awardee, is known for his role in negotiating which international convention?
  1. 1. Kyoto Protocol
  2. 2. Paris Agreement
  3. 3. Convention on Biological Diversity
  4. 4. UN Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS)
Answer: 4 — The Singaporean diplomat presided over the conference that finalised UNCLOS. The other 2026 awardees were Runa Khan (Bangladesh) and Bo Kyi (Myanmar).
Q.48
Who received the Lokmanya Tilak National Award on 1 August 2026?
  1. 1. S. Jaishankar
  2. 2. Nitin Gadkari
  3. 3. Ajit Doval
  4. 4. Sudha Murty
Answer: 3 — National Security Adviser Ajit Doval received the award on Tilak's death anniversary.
Q.49

  1. 1. Haraprasad Das
  2. 2. Girijakumar Baliyar Singh
  3. 3. Rajendra Kishore Panda
  4. 4. Pratibha Ray
Answer: 2 — Girijakumar Baliyar Singh won the 2025 Sahitya Akademi Award in Odia for 'Padapurana', which blends spiritual themes with contemporary social reflection.
Q.50
Which phrase describes the theme of India's BRICS chairship and the 18th BRICS Summit (New Delhi, September 2026)?
  1. 1. Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability
  2. 2. Strengthening Global South Cooperation for Inclusive Governance
  3. 3. BRICS and Africa: Partnership for Accelerated Growth
  4. 4. One Earth, One Family, One Future
Answer: 1 — India's theme spells out BRICS (Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation, Sustainability). It was India's fourth chairship.
Q.51
The term of which UN Secretary-General ends on 31 December 2026?
  1. 1. Ban Ki-moon
  2. 2. António Guterres
  3. 3. Kofi Annan
  4. 4. Amina J. Mohammed
Answer: 2 — António Guterres (Portugal) has served since January 2017. The selection of his successor began in November 2025.
Q.52
SLINEX 2026, a bilateral naval exercise hosted at Visakhapatnam, was conducted between India and which country?
  1. 1. Singapore
  2. 2. Sri Lanka
  3. 3. Saudi Arabia
  4. 4. Seychelles
Answer: 2 — SLINEX = Sri Lanka–India Naval Exercise. The India–Seychelles exercise is LAMITIYE, and India–Singapore is SIMBEX.
Q.53
LAMITIYE-2026, which featured India's first tri-services participation in that series, is a joint military exercise between India and which country?
  1. 1. Mauritius
  2. 2. Maldives
  3. 3. Seychelles
  4. 4. Sri Lanka
Answer: 3 — LAMITIYE ('friendship' in Seychellois Creole) is held with Seychelles. Its 11th edition was held at the Seychelles Defence Academy in 2026.
Q.54
General Dhiraj Seth took charge as Chief of the Army Staff on 30 June 2026, succeeding whom?
  1. 1. General Manoj Pande
  2. 2. General Upendra Dwivedi
  3. 3. General Anil Chauhan
  4. 4. General M.M. Naravane
Answer: 2 — Gen Seth became the 31st COAS after Gen Upendra Dwivedi retired. Gen Anil Chauhan is the Chief of Defence Staff.
Q.55
Who is the Governor of the Reserve Bank of India (as of October 2026)?
  1. 1. Sanjay Malhotra
  2. 2. Shaktikanta Das
  3. 3. Urjit Patel
  4. 4. T. Rabi Sankar

Q.56

  1. 1. 5.50%
  2. 2. 6.00%
  3. 3. 5.25%
  4. 4. 5.00%
Answer: 3 — The repo rate stayed at 5.25% (after the December 2025 cut) through the first half of 2026, before a hike to 5.50% in October 2026.
Q.57
What was India's rank in WIPO's Global Innovation Index 2025?
  1. 1. 39th
  2. 2. 38th
  3. 3. 40th
  4. 4. 32nd
Answer: 2 — India rose from 39th (2024) to 38th (2025) and leads the Central and Southern Asia region.
Q.58

  1. 1. 99th
  2. 2. 109th
  3. 3. 94th
  4. 4. 112th
Answer: 3 — India ranked 94th of 167 countries (score 68.3), up from 99th in 2025 and 112th in 2015.
Q.59

  1. 1. It improved from 135th
  2. 2. It declined from 115th
  3. 3. It was unchanged
  4. 4. It improved from 140th
Answer: 2 — India fell from 115th to 127th, mainly because of border tensions and continuing violence in Manipur.

Q.62

  1. 1. N.T. Rama Rao
  2. 2. Alluri Sitarama Raju
  3. 3. Potti Sriramulu
  4. 4. Tanguturi Prakasam

Q.63
Which launch vehicle and humanoid robot are planned for ISRO's first uncrewed Gaganyaan mission (G1)?
  1. 1. PSLV carrying Pragyan
  2. 2. GSLV Mk II carrying Vikram
  3. 3. SSLV carrying Vyommitra
  4. 4. Human-rated LVM3 carrying Vyommitra
Answer: 4 — The human-rated LVM3 will carry the crew module with the half-humanoid robot Vyommitra. Crewed flights are planned for 2027.
Q.64
For the development of which materials did Kitagawa, Robson and Yaghi receive the Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2025?
  1. 1. Quantum dots
  2. 2. Lithium-ion batteries
  3. 3. Metal–organic frameworks (MOFs)
  4. 4. Click chemistry
Answer: 3 — MOFs are porous crystalline materials used, for example, to capture CO₂ or harvest water from air. Click chemistry won in 2022.
Q.65
What is the reference date for the population enumeration of Census 2027 in most of India (other than snow-bound areas)?
  1. 1. 1 April 2026
  2. 2. 1 March 2027
  3. 3. 1 October 2026
  4. 4. 1 January 2027
Answer: 2 — The reference date is 00:00 hours on 1 March 2027. For snow-bound areas such as Ladakh and parts of J&K, Himachal and Uttarakhand, it is 1 October 2026.
