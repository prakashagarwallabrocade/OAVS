Q.41
By winning the Australian Open 2026, Carlos Alcaraz completed a career Grand Slam. Whom did he defeat in the final?
  1. 1. Jannik Sinner
  2. 2. Alexander Zverev
  3. 3. Daniil Medvedev
  4. 4. Novak Djokovic
Answer: 4 — Alcaraz beat Novak Djokovic 2–6, 6–2, 6–3, 7–5 to win his first Australian Open, having already won the French Open, Wimbledon and the US Open.
Q.42
Which team won the ICC Women's T20 World Cup 2026, beating England in the final at Lord's?
  1. 1. India
  2. 2. South Africa
  3. 3. New Zealand
  4. 4. Australia
Answer: 4 — Australia beat hosts England by seven wickets on 6 July 2026 for a record seventh Women's T20 World Cup title. Beth Mooney scored 64.
Q.43
Which country won the Uber Cup (women's world team badminton championship) in 2026 at Horsens, Denmark?
  1. 1. China
  2. 2. South Korea
  3. 3. Japan
  4. 4. Indonesia
Answer: 2 — South Korea beat defending champions China 3–1 for their third Uber Cup. China retained the men's Thomas Cup by beating France.
Q.44
At the 46th Chess Olympiad (2026) held in Samarkand, what did India win?
  1. 1. Gold in both sections
  2. 2. Silver in the Open section and bronze in the Women's section
  3. 3. Gold in the Open section and silver in the Women's section
  4. 4. Bronze in the Open section only
Answer: 2 — Hosts Uzbekistan won the Open gold, with India second. China won the Women's gold, and India took bronze on tie-break.
Q.45
The FIDE Chess World Cup 2025, won by 19-year-old Javokhir Sindarov, was held in which Indian state?
  1. 1. Tamil Nadu
  2. 2. Kerala
  3. 3. Goa
  4. 4. Gujarat
Answer: 3 — Goa hosted the FIDE World Cup from 31 October to 27 November 2025. The event returned to India after 23 years.
Q.46
Which country topped the medal table at the Milan–Cortina 2026 Winter Olympics?
  1. 1. United States
  2. 2. Italy
  3. 3. Germany
  4. 4. Norway
Answer: 4 — Norway finished first with 18 gold and 41 medals in all. The USA was second, and hosts Italy won 30 medals.
Q.47
Who led the Indian contingent as flag-bearers at the opening ceremony of the Asian Games 2026 in Aichi–Nagoya?
  1. 1. Neeraj Chopra and P.V. Sindhu
  2. 2. Manu Bhaker and Pawan Sehrawat
  3. 3. Harmanpreet Singh and Lovlina Borgohain
  4. 4. Manu Bhaker and Neeraj Chopra
Answer: 2 — Shooter Manu Bhaker and kabaddi star Pawan Sehrawat carried the flag. Sehrawat replaced shot-putter Tajinderpal Singh Toor at the last minute.
Q.48
Which two of the five Padma Vibhushan awards announced in January 2026 were conferred posthumously?
  1. 1. Dharmendra and V.S. Achuthanandan
  2. 2. N. Rajam and K.T. Thomas
  3. 3. Dharmendra and N. Rajam
  4. 4. P. Narayanan and V.S. Achuthanandan
Answer: 1 — Actor Dharmendra and former Kerala CM V.S. Achuthanandan were honoured posthumously. The other recipients were violinist N. Rajam, Justice K.T. Thomas and P. Narayanan.
Q.49
The 60th Jnanpith Award (for 2025) went to a writer in which language?
  1. 1. Malayalam
  2. 2. Tamil
  3. 3. Hindi
  4. 4. Kannada
Answer: 2 — Tamil poet and lyricist R. Vairamuthu received it. He is the first to be honoured for Tamil poetry and the third Tamil writer overall.
Q.50
Anant Nag, chosen for the Dadasaheb Phalke Award for 2024, is the second actor from which film industry to receive it?
  1. 1. Telugu
  2. 2. Malayalam
  3. 3. Kannada
  4. 4. Marathi
Answer: 3 — Anant Nag is the second Kannada actor to receive the Phalke Award, after Dr Rajkumar (1995).
Q.51
Consider the following statements about the Union Budget 2026-27.
1. The fiscal deficit target is 4.3% of GDP.
2. The government aims to bring the debt-to-GDP ratio down to around 50% by March 2031.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
  1. 1. Only 1
  2. 2. Only 2
  3. 3. Both 1 and 2
  4. 4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: 3 — Both are correct. Outstanding liabilities are estimated at 55.6% of GDP in 2026-27, with the aim of reaching about 50% by March 2031.
Q.52
What real GDP growth range for 2026-27 was projected by the Economic Survey 2025-26?
  1. 1. 6.3%–6.8%
  2. 2. 7.5%–8.0%
  3. 3. 6.8%–7.2%
  4. 4. 5.8%–6.2%
Answer: 3 — The Survey, prepared under CEA V. Anantha Nageswaran, projected 6.8–7.2% growth, with India remaining the fastest-growing major economy.
Q.53
Consider the following statements about Census 2027.
1. It is India's first digital census, with an option of online self-enumeration.
2. Its first phase (House Listing and Housing Census) began on 1 April 2026.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
  1. 1. Only 1
  2. 2. Only 2
  3. 3. Both 1 and 2
  4. 4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer: 3 — Both are correct. House-listing ran from April to September 2026, each state taking a 30-day window preceded by 15 days of self-enumeration.
Q.54
In the Human Development Report 2025, India ranked 130th with an HDI value of 0.685. In which category does this place India?
  1. 1. High human development
  2. 2. Low human development
  3. 3. Very high human development
  4. 4. Medium human development
Answer: 4 — India is in the 'medium' category. The threshold for 'high' human development is an HDI of 0.700.
Q.55
In the Global Hunger Index 2025, India ranked 102nd out of 123 countries. How is its hunger level classified?
  1. 1. Serious
  2. 2. Moderate
  3. 3. Alarming
  4. 4. Low
Answer: 1 — With a score of 25.8, India's hunger level is 'serious'. It ranked 105th in 2024.
Q.56
The 52nd G7 Summit in June 2026 was held at Évian-les-Bains in which country?
  1. 1. Switzerland
  2. 2. France
  3. 3. Italy
  4. 4. Germany
Answer: 2 — France hosted it at Évian on Lake Geneva. Évian also hosted the G8 in 2003.
Q.57
Which city hosted the 2026 SCO Summit, marking 25 years of the organisation?
  1. 1. Tianjin
  2. 2. Astana
  3. 3. Bishkek
  4. 4. Dushanbe
Answer: 3 — Kyrgyzstan hosted it in Bishkek (31 August–1 September 2026). Tianjin (China) hosted the 2025 summit.
Q.58
The UN General Assembly has declared 2026 as the International Year of which of the following?
  1. 1. Millets
  2. 2. Camelids
  3. 3. Rangelands and Pastoralists
  4. 4. Glaciers' Preservation
Answer: 3 — 2026 is the International Year of Rangelands and Pastoralists (also of Women Farmers, and of Volunteers). Millets was 2023, camelids 2024 and glaciers' preservation 2025.
Q.59
C. Joseph Vijay, sworn in as Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu on 10 May 2026, heads which party?
  1. 1. Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam
  2. 2. Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam
  3. 3. All India Anna DMK
  4. 4. Naam Tamilar Katchi
Answer: 1 — TVK leader Vijay formed a coalition government with the Congress, ending decades of DMK–AIADMK rule.
Q.60
V.D. Satheesan became Chief Minister of Kerala in May 2026 as the UDF returned to power after how many years?
  1. 1. 5 years
  2. 2. 15 years
  3. 3. 20 years
  4. 4. 10 years
Answer: 4 — The LDF had ruled Kerala for two consecutive terms (2016–2026). The Congress-led UDF returned after 10 years.
Q.61
Who became Prime Minister of Bangladesh after the BNP won the February 2026 general election?
  1. 1. Muhammad Yunus
  2. 2. Mirza Fakhrul Islam Alamgir
  3. 3. Tarique Rahman
  4. 4. Khaleda Zia
Answer: 3 — Tarique Rahman, son of Khaleda Zia and Ziaur Rahman, became PM. It was the first election since the 2024 uprising, and the Awami League was barred.
Q.62
Balendra (Balen) Shah, who became Nepal's youngest democratically elected Prime Minister in March 2026, leads which party?
  1. 1. Nepali Congress
  2. 2. Rastriya Swatantra Party
  3. 3. CPN (UML)
  4. 4. CPN (Maoist Centre)
Answer: 2 — The RSP won 182 of 275 seats in the House of Representatives, and Shah was sworn in on 27 March 2026.
Q.63
Noida International Airport, inaugurated on 28 March 2026, is located at Jewar in which district?
  1. 1. Ghaziabad
  2. 2. Aligarh
  3. 3. Bulandshahr
  4. 4. Gautam Buddh Nagar
Answer: 4 — Jewar lies in Gautam Buddh Nagar district of Uttar Pradesh.
Q.64
Under the GST rate rationalisation in force from 22 September 2025, what is the special rate applied to luxury and 'sin' goods?
  1. 1. 28%
  2. 2. 40%
  3. 3. 35%
  4. 4. 50%
Answer: 2 — Most goods now fall into two main slabs, 5% and 18%, with a special 40% rate for luxury and sin goods.
Q.65
The Nobel Prize in Physics 2025 (Clarke, Devoret and Martinis) was awarded for discoveries related to which of the following?
  1. 1. Attosecond light pulses
  2. 2. Artificial neural networks
  3. 3. Gravitational waves
  4. 4. Macroscopic quantum tunnelling in an electric circuit
Answer: 4 — Their experiments showed quantum effects in a circuit large enough to hold in the hand, the basis of superconducting qubits.
Q.66
Under the arrangement for COP31 (2026), which country hosts the conference in Antalya, and which country chairs the negotiations?
  1. 1. Australia hosts; Türkiye chairs the negotiations
  2. 2. Brazil hosts; Türkiye chairs the negotiations
  3. 3. Türkiye hosts; Brazil chairs the negotiations
  4. 4. Türkiye hosts; Australia chairs the negotiations
Answer: 4 — COP31 has the first-ever split presidency: Türkiye hosts and holds the presidency, while Australia's minister is President of Negotiations.
Q.67
With which country did India sign a Free Trade Agreement in New Delhi on 28 April 2026?
  1. 1. Australia
  2. 2. New Zealand
  3. 3. Canada
  4. 4. Oman
Answer: 2 — The India–New Zealand FTA was signed in April 2026, three months after India and the EU concluded their FTA.
Q.68

  1. 1. Keonjhar
  2. 2. Sundargarh
  3. 3. Balasore
  4. 4. Mayurbhanj

Q.69
Under the 'Samruddha Odisha' vision, what economy size does Odisha target by 2036, the centenary of its formation as a province?
  1. 1. US $1 trillion
  2. 2. US $250 billion
  3. 3. US $1.5 trillion
  4. 4. US $500 billion
Answer: 4 — Odisha targets $500 billion by 2036, and $1.5 trillion by 2047.
Q.70
Who is the Speaker of the 17th Odisha Legislative Assembly (as of October 2026)?
  1. 1. Surama Padhy
  2. 2. Pramila Mallik
  3. 3. Pravati Parida
  4. 4. Bikram Keshari Arukha
Answer: 1 — Surama Padhy (BJP) was elected Speaker in June 2024. Pramila Mallik (2022–24) was the first woman Speaker of the Assembly, and Pravati Parida is a Deputy Chief Minister.
